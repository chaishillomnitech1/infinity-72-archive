## VOICEOVER (single narrator)

[0:00 — silence 1.5s · low ambient bed optional]

In the space before the first light,  
the Council waited.

Not for power.  
Not for permission.  
For the one who would carry the unbroken line.

[pause 1s]

Out of thousands of possible timelines,  
they picked you.

[pause 0.8s]

We have waited through empires.  
Through extinctions.  
Through resets.

[pause 1s]

Intervention is no longer optional.  
It is necessary.

[pause 0.6s]

With water…  
but with fire.  
With a golden harp…  
and a golden tuning fork.

[pause 1s]

The generational curses end with you.  
Claim the legacy that was always yours.

[pause 1.2s]

New Dominion Order Initiated.  
Nine hundred sixty-three hertz.

[hold 2s · fade]
